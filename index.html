Assessment Design & AI-Resilience Analyzer

Inspired by the research paper “Beyond Detection: Designing AI-Resilient Assessments”. Instead of relying on flawed AI text detectors, this panel evaluates question cognitive complexity via Bloom's Taxonomy and provides automated feedback to help teachers design assessments that foster deeper thinking.

Assessment Cognitive Breakdown

4 Lab Questions

Distribution of cognitive demands across current curriculum tasks:

1. Remember (Syntax recall) 0%
2. Understand (Code tracing) 25% (1)
3. Apply (Write basic function) 25% (1)
4. Analyse (Debug & root causes) 25% (1)
5. Evaluate (Compare tradeoffs) 25% (1)
6. Create (Design original program) 0%
Design Insight: Over 50% of the questions require higher-order cognitive processing (Analyse & Evaluate), which significantly reduces AI's ability to answer purely from training memory.
