Assessment Design & AI-Resilience Analyzer

Grounded in the University of Sydney research (“Beyond Detection: Designing AI-Resilient Assessments”). Rather than relying on unreliable AI text detectors that produce false accusations, this tool guides educators to design cognitively demanding tasks across Bloom’s Taxonomy that are inherently resistant to AI completion.

Assessment Cognitive Breakdown

4 Semester 1 Labs

Distribution of cognitive demands across current curriculum tasks:

1. Remember (Syntax recall) 0% (0)
2. Understand (Code tracing & explanation) 25% (1)
3. Apply (Write basic function) 25% (1)
4. Analyse (Debug logic & explain root causes) 25% (1)
5. Evaluate (Compare tradeoffs & efficiency) 25% (1)
6. Create (Design original program) 0% (0)
Pedagogical Recommendation: Over 50% of the course questions assess higher-order thinking (Analyse & Evaluate). This structure ensures students build authentic debugging skills rather than relying on generative AI shortcuts.
